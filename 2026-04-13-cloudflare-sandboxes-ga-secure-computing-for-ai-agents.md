# Cloudflare Sandboxes GA: Secure Computing for AI Agents

## Summary
Cloudflare has announced the General Availability (GA) of its Sandboxes, offering AI agents dedicated, secure, and persistent computing environments. These sandboxes provide a complete computational context with a shell, filesystem, and background processes, enabling agents to execute code, manage repositories, and debug effectively. The feature addresses the need for AI agents to operate beyond simple prompts, providing an isolated and stateful environment that starts on demand and resumes exactly where it left off.

## Key Insights
- Cloudflare Sandboxes provide AI agents with a 'real computer' environment, including a shell, filesystem, and background processes, moving beyond traditional prompt-based interactions.
- The sandboxes are persistent and isolated, allowing agents to retain their state between sessions and resume work on demand from where they left off.
- AI agents can perform development-oriented tasks within the sandbox, such as cloning repositories, running Python/JavaScript code, and debugging via a real PTY terminal.
- The solution emphasizes providing a secure and dedicated environment for AI agent operations, enhancing their capabilities and reliability.
